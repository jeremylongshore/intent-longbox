## 1. Why principles, and why now

- Longbox sits between an owner, the employees who will use it all day, the walk-in sellers whose collections it photographs, and buyers who trust a condition line. The product records who did what, how fast, and how often they were wrong — exactly the data that becomes a covert speed quota if nobody says it may not (014 §17 "worker surveillance or deskilling").
- Two locked decisions already exist (condition is never numeric; nothing publishes without a human) and two council amendments (no metric fed by a staff action; every non-waivable line has a live detector). This record turns them, plus the accessibility and privacy commitments the journeys assume, into a single ratified set that E05-B02 (design system and copy), E08-B01 (condition policy), E11-B09 (support/break-glass) and E16-B03 (training) cite instead of re-deriving.
- The cannon found that the shipped UI already breaks P6 (percentages on screen, `public/app.js:103,113`) and that the draft promised three things no detector covers (bystanders, buyer data, provider-side deletion). Under 019 amendment 2 an undetected promise is a waiver by omission, so this revision states, for every principle, what is enforced by a detector, what is a capture-side control, and what is a contractual representation.

## 2. Principles (proposed)

Each principle ends with an **Enforcement** line using the 019 v1.2.0 vocabulary: `detector` = a runtime check that fails closed and emits a heartbeat on T34's list (019 amendment 2); `gate-test` = a CI or one-shot check — never a substitute for a detector on a non-waivable line; `control` = a capture-side or runbook control; `contract` = a representation in the pilot agreement. **Claimable as (021 B16):** only a `detector` line may ever be spoken externally as an achievement, and only once TESTED with its heartbeat green; `gate-test`, `control` and `contract` lines are commitments.

### P1 — Humans hold authority over identity, condition and publishing
- The system proposes; a person confirms every identity (one tap is still a tap), calls every condition, and publishes every listing. No path exists for the software to do any of the three alone (T7, T19; CLAUDE.md locked decisions 3 and 5).
- A contradiction between evidence and candidate always removes the one-tap path (locked decision 7). The system never argues with a correction: a human pick is the truth of record for that scan, and the machine's pick is kept as history, never overwritten (004 §Journey 3; 018 C5).
- Condition is a grade range plus named defects, in the employee's plain words. No numeric grade exists in schema, API, prompt, listing or screen, and Longbox never describes itself as grading (T7; E08-B01 wording is the ceiling).
- **A wrong machine call is a system defect, never an employee fault.** A dispute traced to a good-faith confirmed proposal is recorded as a system defect (E11-B04) and is not grounds for discipline (cannon hr F7).
- Enforcement: detector (T19 — watcher on T34's list); gate-test (T7 numeric-grade scan — not on T34's heartbeat list, so "no numeric grade" is a commitment, not a claimable achievement, until it is); contract (the blame clause, E01-B05 [counsel]).

### P2 — Overrides are always available, cheap, recorded, and never punished
- Every suggested identity, condition and price can be overridden by the person in front of the book. The override is one action on the screen that showed the suggestion ("Not this one — show other matches"), never a new screen or a form; the reason is one tap or one line (E09-B08, E11-B03).
- **Undo is post-hoc, never a confirm dialog.** Every consequential action offers a same-screen undo ("Condition saved. Undo") that **persists until the next capture starts, never less than 15 seconds** — a five-second countdown is a reflex tax on someone holding a book one-handed with a queue behind them (VP DevRel) — plus a "fix the last item" path from the next screen. A pre-action "Are you sure?" is forbidden anywhere in the capture flow, including override, condition and price, because it taxes every book to protect a few (T10). Because records are append-only, undo writes a superseding record; it never edits history (P1; 018 C5). Today there is no back path from condition or price, and `condition_assessment` / `pricing_snapshot` have no `supersedes_id` — `longbox-e5b.2.11` reserves the columns and the current-record read model; E05-B09 builds undo on them.
- Overrides and corrections are recorded because they are the learning signal (017 P18), not because they are evidence against the person. No individual correction count is ever a performance measure (P3).
- Enforcement: gate-test (E05-B09 acceptance: override and undo reachable from every suggestion screen; no confirm dialog in the flow); the no-punishment half is contract (Q2 covenant).

### P3 — Staff data serves learning and support, never covert measurement
- Per-person telemetry (minutes per item, corrections, abstentions, band outcomes) exists to calibrate the system, to train, and to fix support problems — nothing else.
- **No individual-level telemetry view — for the employee, the owner or Longbox — exists before E03-B02 (authentication) and E03-B03 (RBAC + actor audit) are TESTED at G2.** Today `created_by` is client-supplied and unauthenticated (016 C4); a per-person number attributed on that basis would be both unsafe and wrong. Until G2 only shift/batch aggregates render, and no per-person attribution is stored as authoritative (cannon security F5).
- Pre-G2 data is aggregate-only **by construction, not by policy**: no row written before G2 is attributable to a person and none is ever backfilled (CTO). After G2 the employee sees their own record by default (Q3) — their corrections, abstentions and band outcomes, and minutes-per-item only alongside the registered copy 021 C6 (*"…It is not a target, Longbox does not report it to anyone as a target, and it never compares you to anyone."* — a vendor cannot promise what an employer's pay scheme does). The self-view renders **only inside the employee's own authenticated session**: never in the scan flow, never as a panel or drill-down from the owner's console, never on a shared or kiosk device without fresh authentication. The owner cannot switch it off. The owner sees aggregates by shift and batch, never a ranking. The self-view ships in the same bead that ships the first per-person surface for anyone (E03-B03 / E11-B06) — symmetry by construction.
- **Symmetry:** any per-employee view the owner can open, that employee can open about themselves. Break-glass is the **only** technical read path to per-operator data — a distinct named role, not a policy overlay on an admin session (CISO). A break-glass view of an individual's record notifies that employee within 7 days; if it concerns an investigation of them by the shop the notice is deferred — logged at deferral with reason and expiry, delivered no later than 30 days after the investigation closes, never open-ended, never waived (GC).
- **T35 (019 v1.2.0, non-waivable): per-operator rendering outside break-glass = 0.** As entered in 019: (a) *detector* — a runtime per-response assertion that no response body or export carries an operator identifier alongside more than one `scan_session_id`, no export, report or template carries an operator column outside the break-glass role regardless of session count, and no route accepts an operator/`created_by` value as a filter, sort or grouping parameter, outside the break-glass role; heartbeat on T34. (b) *gate-tests* — `created_by`/`operator_id` selectable only inside one audited accessor module (OPEN: none exists at `1ed9ffb`, E03-B03 owns it), and a CI walk of Fastify's registered route table that fails closed on any unclassified route. (c) *completeness* — periodic reconciliation of Longbox-origin database sessions against break-glass rows; an unmatched session is K1, because the Pilot A manual 006 runbook is self-attested and evidence of nothing without it (CISO). (a) and the route-walk are OPEN until `operator_id` exists (E03-B03) and render OPEN on any citing artifact; the cheapest way to satisfy T35 is never to build a per-operator surface, and no such surface may be built and then restricted (CFO).
- No speed quota, leaderboard or per-employee target is built into the product or reported by it. The north star and every threshold in 019 §3.3 divide by paid hours, not by person (019 amendment 1).
- **Longbox is not a timekeeping, payroll or wage-and-hour system.** The `labor_shift` window (E01-B02, E02-B03) is a denominator for the shop's own metric, not a record of hours worked; the shop keeps its own timekeeping, reconciles in its favor, and pays for all time worked. `labor_shift` rows have **their own retention window — 24 months from shift end** (not the derivative window: a labor record's lifetime must not depend on a Shopify listing's; CTO) — and their own hold exemption (Q6). [counsel] (cannon hr F4)
- **Monitoring notice:** before first use each employee receives a one-page plain-language notice of what Longbox records about them, who can see it and how. It is a **G1 artifact alongside T33**, versioned and effective-dated, re-acknowledged on any material change; Pilot A may not start against an unversioned notice; the shop keeps the acknowledgement and **Longbox retains a copy** (a record only the shop holds cannot evidence our own compliance claim — GC). It is written against the named instruments (NY Civil Rights Law §52-c, CT Gen. Stat. §31-48d, 19 Del. C. §705) even though the pilot is in Florida, so the same notice travels (CSO). Registered as 021 C10. Biometric templates of any kind remain on the never list. [counsel] (cannon hr F6)
- Longbox never sells, shares or benchmarks employee-level data across shops; cross-shop learning uses aggregate, de-identified, consented data only (E01-B06).
- Enforcement: detector (T35(a), T34 heartbeat); gate-test (T35(b), G2 gate on E03-B02/B03); contract (Q2 covenant, timekeeping disclaimer, monitoring notice — all [counsel]).

### P4 — Deskilling is a failure mode; the tool amplifies the person
- The employee is not expected to know grading scales, pricing sources or Shopify (004 §Personas). The tool asks for what they know — "what book is in your hand", "what does it look like" — and never for a judgment it should make itself.
- When the system abstains, the fallback is a search-and-pick flow the employee already understands, never a dead end (004 §avoid).
- Training is measured as time-to-first-clean-batch and inter-rater consistency (019 T8), used to fix the training, never to fail the person; T8 is internal-only and never a claim about a person's competence.
- Enforcement: control (E16-B03 training design); T8 internal-only is a 021 blocklist row (B5). **Claimable as commitment only, never achievement** (CMO: keep it — it answers "is this replacing my staff" — but it is unfalsifiable and stays out of every claims artifact as measured).

### P5 — Benefit sharing is stated, not assumed
- The pilot agreement (E01-B05/B07) states in writing what the shop gets (listed inventory, labor hours back, a report), what Longbox gets (confirmations, corrections, a case study only with consent), and that neither side's staff time is free: the shop's operator hours are the T11 denominator.
- If Longbox's learning from a shop's corrections becomes a product asset, that shop's continued price reflects it — the mechanism is a decision for E12-B08/E19-B02, but the principle is fixed here: contributors of training signal are not charged as if they contributed nothing.
- **Open item (E12-B08):** as drafted the benefit flows to the shop, not to the employees who generate the signal. E12-B08 must answer whether and how employees see any of it **before the design-partner wave**, and "no direct employee benefit at pilot scale" is an acceptable answer so the item cannot ratchet (CFO). Until answered it is stated plainly in the one-pager or omitted from it — never paraphrased into an implied promise (GC).
- Enforcement: contract.

### P6 — Accessible by default, plain language always
- **Conformance target: WCAG 2.2 Level AA per WCAG §5 conformance requirements** for every Longbox-authored view in the process from shop-select through draft-created, evaluated as full pages and as a complete process (CR3), by WCAG-EM against the **complete** AA success-criteria set including the 2.2 additions (2.4.11, 2.5.7, 2.5.8, 3.2.6, 3.3.7, 3.3.8 — the last binds E03-B02). Platform camera and file-picker UI are covered by a **statement of partial conformance for third-party content**, not declared out of scope; the control that invokes them must itself conform, and the flow must not dead-end on them — an alternate upload path reachable without the platform picker exists (CSO, VP DevRel). Desktop by the design-partner wave.
- **Sequencing and acceptance (Q5; folds into E14-B06).** *G2 blocker:* the `%` copy defect (`longbox-e5b.5.11`), the six known failures (2.1.1 keyboard reachability — `public/app.js:130-140` unreachable `div onclick`; 4.1.2 name/role/value — candidates become `role="radio"` in a `role="radiogroup"`; 4.1.3 status messages — `#status` gets `role="status"`; 1.4.1 colour never the only band signal; 2.5.8 44 px targets; 1.4.11 non-text contrast) fixed under E05-B02, and gate (a): axe-core over every state (start, photo, high, medium, low/manual, condition, price, draft, error), zero serious/critical, in CI, on the same device matrix T10 uses. *G3 blocker, still before Pilot A:* the full WCAG-EM Level AA evaluation, the partial-conformance statement, an internally published ACR, and gate (c): a one-handed assistive run — iPhone SE, VoiceOver, thumb only, 1.5 Mbps / 300 ms RTT, photo → draft completed unaided, **one device, one run, time-boxed to four hours, run by someone who did not build the screen** (CFO, VP DevRel); Android repeats at the design-partner wave. The run's time is recorded and never gated on (P3).
- Copy is retail language ("looks Fine to Very Fine, spine ticks, corner wear"), never probability language and never a bare number pretending to be a grade (004 §Journey 1; 019 §2 applies to screens). **This record fixes the meaning of band copy** — band name only, no number, plain retail words, action first, contradiction named in one line — and **the exact strings live in 021 C1–C3**, revisable by a 006 row without reopening this record (VP DevRel).
- **The shipped UI violates this today:** `public/app.js:103` renders "(NN%)" on the status line and `:113` appends "[NN%]" to every candidate. Ratification carries the fix bead `longbox-e5b.5.11` (P1, under E05) so P6 does not ship pre-violated (cannon ux F1).
- Errors say what to do next; a screen never blames the person.
- Enforcement: gate-test (axe in CI; the `%`-in-copy CI grep from `longbox-e5b.5.11` onward; the WCAG-EM evaluation). The ACR is the durable artifact; **no public or partner artifact says "WCAG 2.2 AA" before the ACR exists** — until then the sayable form is "we are building to WCAG 2.2 AA" (021 B13, T26).

### P7 — Privacy of people in the frame and behind the counter
- **What the record holds.** Event rows never contain image bytes; `scan_photo` carries a storage key and a content hash. Deletion tombstones the object and appends a `media_deletion` event: the row survives, the photo does not. This is how T33's deletion path stays testable inside the Hickey model (018 C5; cannon security F10). **NOT-YET-ENFORCED:** at `1ed9ffb` `scan_photo` has `storage_url` only, no `media_deletion` table exists, and the append-only trigger forbids DELETE on `scan_photo` (CISO, CTO verified `migrations/001_init.sql:73-80` for the table and `:175-181` for the DELETE-forbidding trigger). `longbox-e5b.2.11` (E02-D01) reserves `storage_key`, `content_hash`, `operator_id` + `actor_verified`, the `media_deletion` table (open-world `reason_code`, idempotent on storage key), listing-lifecycle states and immutable hold rows in one migration **before any pilot row exists**; this sentence carries the NOT-YET-ENFORCED label until that bead closes.
- **Originals are private.** Longbox strips EXIF on ingest (E05-B07), stores originals behind auth — never a public directory (016 C3; today `uploads/` is a public static mount, one of the eight 019 §3.0 safety controls) — and serves them by signed, scoped URL (E03-B07, E13-B06).
- **Retention windows and holds (Q6):** originals — 30 days after the `shopify_draft` is created, hard ceiling 90 days from capture; derivatives (the listing images) — life of the listing plus 90 days, where "life of the listing" is computed from an observed lifecycle event (the T19 status watcher extended with `delisted`/`archived`), **hard ceiling 24 months from capture**, exported to the shop at offboarding; `labor_shift` — 24 months from shift end (P3); immutable event rows — never deleted. Per-shop configuration may shorten any window, never lengthen it. **Holds:** any item under an open dispute, chargeback, return, correction review, litigation or a P9 complaint is exempt from every sweep — originals, derivatives and `labor_shift` alike. A hold is per-item, is itself an immutable row naming reason and review date, is recorded in 006 with scope, trigger and release, is enumerated with its age by the T32 sweep (a sweep is a no-op on held keys and writes its own receipt), escalates as a 006 row past 180 days from capture, and a hold with no review date is a T32 failure (five seats; CISO, CTO wording). Retention-policy changes are themselves immutable rows.
- **Provider copies.** "Deletes on request" applies to Longbox-controlled storage. Copies held by a vision or pricing provider are governed by the signed processor term whose retention window and expiry are recorded in the processor registry (E04-B05); the shop-facing wording **names the provider and its window** (021 B15), and Longbox never claims a deletion it does not control or verify (cannon security F4; CISO). **The pilot is capped at two external processors**; a third is a 006 decision with its own counsel line (CFO).
- **Walk-in sellers** are told, before the shutter, that books they sell are photographed for listing and processed by a third-party service — per 019 T33 (cited, not restated): a counter placard and one pre-printed line on the existing buy slip, never a signature ceremony for a purchase. The buy-slip line is the consent record; it is per batch and revocable. **Revocation deletes stored originals and unpublished derivatives within 5 business days — the same clock as bystander frames, one clock the counter staff can recite** (VP DevRel); an image already published as listing media is replaced, not retroactively unpublished (GC).
- **Bystanders and staff in frame** are a capture-side control, not a detector: the photo recipe frames the book only; the shop represents that it does not photograph uninvolved people; a reported frame is deleted within 5 business days as a `media_deletion` event. Hands, badges and staff caught incidentally are never used for monitoring, training material or any external artifact without separate written consent (cannon security F2, hr F8).
- **Buyer data is never ingested.** Sell-through (E12-B02) reads order line quantities, dates and prices only; any buyer-identifying field is dropped at the Shopify boundary. If E12-B02 cannot be built that way it gets its own T-line before G1 (cannon security F3).
- The shop's own data (sales, wages, inventory) is processed only under the signed scope (E01-B06) and never appears in a public or partner artifact without written consent (CLAUDE.md §Governance pilot-data rule).
- **Longbox-side access.** Longbox operators see shop data only through audited, time-boxed support access; there is no invisible super-admin (E11-B09). E11-B09 lands at G3 and Pilot A precedes it, so at Pilot A every Longbox access to shop data is recorded as a 006 row naming operator, reason and expiry (a manual break-glass runbook), evidenced at least once before G3. The access-audit writer joins T34's heartbeat list (cannon security F7, F8).
- Enforcement: detector (T32 retention sweep incl. hold enumeration, T33 consent gate, T34 heartbeat incl. the access-audit writer, T35(c) reconciliation); gate-test (`media_deletion` tombstone test, once `longbox-e5b.2.11` lands); control (framing recipe, bystander runbook, Pilot A manual break-glass 006 rows); contract (shop representation, processor terms, buy-slip line). Nothing here claims a detector over state Longbox does not own — post-publish listing copy, provider storage, listing life without a lifecycle event are `contract` (CTO).

### P8 — Honesty about what the machine did
- Every listing, report and screen distinguishes what a person decided from what the system proposed. In the phone flow this is a read-only **decision strip** under the header that fills as the session runs — `Book: proposed → you confirmed · Condition: you · Price: policy → you changed` — the same string written to the record and shown in the owner's audit view; nothing on any screen says "AI graded" or "AI priced" (018 §6; cannon ux F6). Where a model was involved in identification, the evidence it used is inspectable by the owner (E06-B08).
- A buyer-facing listing never carries a claim that outruns its evidence (018 A3): condition is the human's words, price is the shop's policy, identity was confirmed by a person. **Provenance sentence (Q7 decision):** the single registered sentence 021 C5 — *"Issue, variant and condition confirmed by a person at this shop; identified with software assistance."* — is default-on in the `productSet` draft template Longbox creates, asserted by a contract test on the draft mutation; the shop may remove it per listing, the removal is recorded and counted, and it never changes the decision-strip record; no shop- or Longbox-invented variant exists (021 B14); Longbox does not claim the sentence survives owner edits after publish — post-publish copy is not our artifact.
- Enforcement: gate-test (decision strip present on every session screen — E05-B09 acceptance; C5 present in the `productSet` payload — contract test); 021 blocklist for the retired words and invented provenance wording.

### P9 — A named path for misuse, and the person's own record
- Anyone who believes Longbox data was used against them has a named path: the owner first, then a Longbox **role** (not a person — a solo founder with no rota cannot promise immediacy; CFO) named in the monitoring notice, with a 10-business-day response window, who **pulls and provides the break-glass record and nothing more** — Longbox does not investigate, adjudicate or opine on any employment decision (GC). Employees can see and request correction of their own record; when they leave, it survives only de-identified (cannon hr F7).
- Enforcement: contract (E01-B05 clause [counsel]; the contact sentence is 021 C11); detector (the break-glass log itself, T35 path).
